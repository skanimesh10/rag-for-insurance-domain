MOTOR POLICY MP-2023-045
Policy Type: Motor

Section 1.0 Coverage Overview
This policy covers own-damage to the insured vehicle and third-party
liability arising from an accident involving the insured vehicle, subject
to the conditions below.

Section 2.0 No Claim Bonus
A no-claim bonus of 20% applies at first renewal if no claims were filed
in the preceding policy year, increasing incrementally up to a maximum of
50% at the fifth consecutive claim-free renewal.

Section 3.0 Waiting Periods
There is no waiting period for own-damage or third-party claims under this
policy; coverage is effective from the policy start date and time stated
on the policy schedule.

Section 4.0 Exclusions
Claims are excluded if the vehicle was being driven without a valid
driving license, under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or for
commercial purposes when the policy is issued for private use only.

Section 5.0 Claims Process
Own-damage claims must be reported within 48 hours of the incident.
A surveyor will be assigned within 24 hours of claim intimation for
damage assessment.
