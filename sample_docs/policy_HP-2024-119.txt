HEALTH POLICY HP-2024-119
Policy Type: Health

Section 1.0 Coverage Overview
This policy provides coverage for hospitalization, outpatient treatment, and
prescribed medication for the policyholder and named dependents, subject to
the terms, conditions, and exclusions described below.

Section 2.0 Sum Insured
The base sum insured under this policy is INR 5,00,000 per policy year.
Restoration of sum insured applies once per policy year if the base amount
is exhausted due to a claim, provided the exhaustion was not due to the same
illness within the same policy year.

Section 3.0 Pre-Existing Conditions
Pre-existing conditions disclosed at the time of proposal are covered after
a waiting period of 36 months from the policy inception date. Conditions
not disclosed at proposal stage are permanently excluded from coverage.

Section 4.0 Waiting Periods
Section 4.1 Initial Waiting Period
All illnesses other than accidents are subject to a 30-day initial waiting
period from the policy start date.

Section 4.2 Maternity Waiting Period
Maternity-related expenses, including normal delivery, cesarean delivery,
and pre/post-natal care, are covered only after a continuous waiting period
of 24 months from the policy inception date. Claims filed before the
waiting period is complete will be denied regardless of medical necessity.

Section 4.3 Specific Illness Waiting Period
Certain named illnesses, including cataract, hernia, and joint replacement
surgery, are subject to a waiting period of 24 months from policy inception.

Section 5.0 Exclusions
The following are excluded from coverage under any circumstance: cosmetic
surgery not resulting from an accident, treatment for self-inflicted
injury, dental treatment unless requiring hospitalization due to an
accident, and any treatment received outside India.

Section 6.0 Claims Process
Claims must be filed within 30 days of discharge from hospital for
reimbursement claims. Cashless claims require pre-authorization at least
48 hours before a planned hospitalization, or within 24 hours of admission
in case of an emergency.
